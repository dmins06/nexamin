## Direction contract

THESIS: Discover a learning lab by field, then enter its independent workspace. One real available lab is clearer than a catalogue of disabled simulations.

OWN-WORLD: Existing Nexamin glass surfaces, semantic dark/light colors, Manrope/Space Grotesk, Lucide icons and visible keyboard focus. No new imagery or global visual system.

STORY: Open Phòng Lab, search or select a field, discover Giải phẫu người 3D, enter the existing viewer and return with context intact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Plain title/subtitle, search, one featured available lab, and a compact field filter showing honest unreleased states. On narrow screens controls stack without changing the five-item bottom bar.

FORM: User-pinned lightweight discovery hub; no concept tournament. Signature interaction: registry-based launch with breadcrumb/back navigation, loading the simulation only after an explicit launch.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
