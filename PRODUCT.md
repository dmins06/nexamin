# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, TypeScript, Vite, Vitest and browser local storage on the frontend; FastAPI, PostgreSQL/pgvector, Redis, Celery and MinIO on the backend.

## Users

Inferred from the brief: university students who need one calm place to turn courses, deadlines, and focus time into a workable daily plan.

## Product Purpose

StudyHubAI helps a student decide what to study now, follow a focused session, and understand whether the week is on track. Success means identifying and beginning the next useful action within seconds.

## Positioning

The product connects a weekly academic plan directly to a focus session and visible study momentum instead of separating tasks, timers, and progress into unrelated tools.

## Operating Context

Primarily a laptop during study blocks, with mobile web for quick progress checks and task updates. The product supports authenticated cloud workflows while retaining local dashboard state for a fast, resilient study experience.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Dashboard overview, today's priorities, weekly progress, focus time, and upcoming work.
- Interactive task completion and focus timer.
- Responsive navigation across overview, courses, document library, AI tutor, schedule, focus and progress.
- Registration with a searchable nationwide catalog of 426 institutions, 426 resilient local institutional images and 6,783 training programs scoped to their offering school.
- Backend APIs for courses, documents, RAG tutor, quiz and study planning.
- Versioned local persistence for dashboard preferences plus authenticated backend workflows.
- Dashboard seed data is illustrative; account, course and document data is persisted by the backend.

## Brand Commitments

The confirmed name is StudyHubAI. Copy is Vietnamese-first, direct, encouraging, and never falsely claims local recommendations came from a live AI model.

## Evidence on Hand

No supplied logo, brand system, customer proof, production data, or third-party assets. Do not invent testimonials, institutional adoption, or outcome claims.

## Product Principles

- Make the next study action unmistakable.
- Show progress as evidence, not pressure.
- Keep planning and doing in the same workflow.
- Keep core dashboard interactions resilient while making connected features explicit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA fundamentals: semantic structure, full keyboard use, visible focus, sufficient contrast, touch-friendly controls, and reduced-motion support.
